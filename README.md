# Last Text at One Percent

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-last-text-at-one-percent-ba9fc876)

Entry for the make.fun competition
[Last Text at One Percent](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-last-text-at-one-percent-ba9fc876):
write the last text a phone sends at 1% battery, under 200 characters,
in the voice of the dying phone itself.

## The text

> 1% left. Tell my charger I loved it. Tell the group chat I died doing
> what I loved: ignoring them. Goodbye, cruel Wi-Fi.

120 characters — one message, no room for a sequel.

## The program

`main.py` is deterministic and needs no network or input. Run it and it
prints exactly that final text:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## Why this text

- **The joke first:** the phone dies mid-drama, handing out last
  wishes like a soap-opera hero — to its charger, and to the group
  chat it spent its life ignoring.
- **The voice:** it is the phone talking, not its owner. It loved the
  charger, not the human. Its final enemy is the Wi-Fi.
- **The economy:** three short sentences, 120 characters, nothing
  wasted — fitting, since the battery is at 1%.
